Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage data — information such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect your bank account details, card numbers, or any data from your other financial accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide your expense records, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers, and passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are permanently removed.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when a page is loaded.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
